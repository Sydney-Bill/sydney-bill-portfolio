How I approach the work

Public finance and development data is dense by nature — my job starts long before the design stage. Whether I'm transcribing a direct interview, cleaning survey data, or analyzing a public finance article for policy insight, I'm looking for the one number or finding that matters most, then building the page, post, or report around making that unmissable.

That means my work spans the full pipeline: research and data collection in the field, technical analysis and reporting at the desk, and finally the visual design, i.e., infographics, reports, and campaign materials that makes it land. Every piece I design carries a consistent system, so a reader recognizes the same disciplined voice whether they're holding a printed brief, reviewing an evaluation report, or scrolling past a social post.
